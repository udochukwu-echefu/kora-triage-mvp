# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Customer support agents and support managers at Nigerian fintech and e-commerce SMBs. Agents need to understand and resolve incoming WhatsApp and email complaints quickly. Managers need to monitor quality, escalation risk, workload, and time savings without losing visibility into individual decisions.

## Product Purpose

Triage realistic customer-support messages by identifying intent, urgency, sentiment, and useful entities; recall prior customer context; route each case to a safe resolution path; draft an appropriate response; and preserve an auditable explanation. Success means faster handling, fewer repeated questions, reliable escalation of risky cases, and measurable classification quality.

## Brand Personality

Calm, accountable, distinctly Nigerian. The voice is direct, respectful, warm without being casual, and comfortable with natural English and Nigerian Pidgin.

## Anti-references

Avoid flashy AI-command-center visuals, neon fintech styling, opaque magic, oversized decorative metrics, excessive cards, and chat interfaces that hide operational detail. Do not make the product feel experimental or crypto-adjacent.

## Design Principles

1. Make the next action obvious for a busy agent.
2. Show the evidence behind every automated decision.
3. Escalate uncertainty visibly and safely.
4. Preserve customer context so the conversation moves forward.
5. Give managers a truthful operational picture, not vanity metrics.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA. Support keyboard navigation, visible focus states, reduced motion, readable contrast, non-color status cues, and responsive layouts. Use plain language while preserving Pidgin in customer messages and appropriate drafted responses.
